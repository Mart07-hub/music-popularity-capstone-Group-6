How the Pairs Work
In each pair, one person leads and the other supports and acts as backup. This
gives us:

•  Continuity if one person cannot finish their part
•  Regular communication between partners, who share responsibility for getting

the task done

•  A shared understanding of every section
•  A smoother final report

Our Agreement
We work in pairs on every major part of the notebook. If one person cannot finish
their share, their partner takes charge and makes sure it is done on time. Every
member contributes properly and understands the work in their sections.
